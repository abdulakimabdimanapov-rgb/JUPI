Player

Movement with acceleration/friction, sprint, dodge with i-frames, 3-hit combo system with finishers, weapon swap, HP regen, level-up system, camera shake.

Enemy AI

State machine: Idle → Chase → Attack → Retreat. 3 enemy types (guard, fast, boss). Status effects (bleed, stun). HP bars, damage numbers, death particles.

Time Travel

Two eras: Present ↔ Past. Blood Clock UI for travel. Past actions weaken the boss. Map regeneration with different tilesets per era.

HUD

HP bar with color states, XP bar, level display, weapon indicator, era indicator, kill counter, objective tracker, combo display.

Audio

Procedural audio synthesis (blips, whooshes, chimes). Supports .ogg/.wav files when available. Sound pooling, looping, random pitch variation.

Input

Full key remapping with save/load to user://keybinds.json. Supports keyboard and mouse bindings.
